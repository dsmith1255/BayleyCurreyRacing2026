Privacy Policy

This policy describes information associated with the Bayley Currey Racing website, sponsorship inquiries, and merchandise store operated by FewShot Solutions.

Information collected

The sponsorship form requests your name, work email, company, partnership interests, and message. You can also provide a phone number and company website. Please do not include payment details or sensitive personal information in an inquiry.

The merchandise integration uses Shopify for checkout. If an order is placed, checkout may collect contact, billing, delivery, order, and payment information. Payment details are entered through Shopify checkout, not through the sponsorship form.

How information is used and processed

Inquiry information is used to respond to your request and discuss potential partnerships. Order information is used to process purchases, arrange fulfillment, communicate about orders, and handle customer service. Hosting and commerce providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure their services.

Netlify hosts the website and processes sponsorship form submissions. Shopify provides commerce and checkout services. The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. The Instagram announcement loads from Instagram when you choose to watch it; Instagram may then process information under its own policies. Other external links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Cookies and browser storage

The shop uses local browser storage to remember cart items. Third-party services may use cookies or other storage when they load. Clearing browser storage can remove a saved cart. You can manage cookies through your browser and choose not to load the Instagram video. This notice does not represent that a site-wide cookie-consent or advertising opt-out system is in place.

Retention and privacy requests

Contact dixon@fewshotsolutions.com to ask about your personal information or request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on your location; some order records may need to be retained for accounting, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

Our detailed retention schedule and provider-specific privacy disclosures are still being reviewed. This notice does not assert that third-party services never share data or that browser privacy signals are automatically honored. See the privacy notices for Netlify, Shopify, and Google for their processing practices.

Changes to this policy will be identified by the updated date above. Any rights provided by applicable law remain unaffected.

Shipping Policy

Orders are expected to ship within 30 calendar days after the order is placed. This timeframe includes production and preparation for shipment; carrier transit time is additional. It is not a promise of delivery within 30 days and is not a return window. Available delivery options, shipping charges, and applicable taxes should be displayed before payment.

If an order cannot ship within the stated timeframe, the customer will be notified of the delay and offered the applicable choice to agree to a revised shipment date or cancel for a refund.

For questions about delivery availability, tracking, or a missing package, contact dixon@fewshotsolutions.com with your order number. Carrier transit estimates and any destination-specific charges are separate from the 30-day shipment timeframe; please ask us before purchasing if a delivery date is essential.
