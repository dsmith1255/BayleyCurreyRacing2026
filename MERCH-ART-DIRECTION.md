# Merch visual direction

Built-in image generation was used for three coordinated product design previews. These are mockups, not photographs of manufactured inventory. The supplied BC monogram and roof-rail SVG files were provided as artwork reference. No stock-photo inversion or CSS logo overlay is used in the final images.

## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium ecommerce product photograph mockup, square composition. Background seamless pale warm grey #e8e6e2, neutral soft broad daylight from upper left, naturally dark black fabric with clear gently lit seams, grounded soft contact shadows. Clean straight-on catalog photography, no red light, no smoke, no gradients or cinematic drama, no pedestal, no border, no captions, no floating decals, no glossy CGI, no exaggerated thread. Full product entirely visible centered, occupies 78% of image width with generous margin. The attached image is artwork reference only, not scene reference. Preserve its actual custom BC monogram shapes and exact BAYLEY CURREY lettering when specified, do not replace with typed approximation. Artwork must be physically integrated on fabric with correct perspective and subtle underlying texture.

## Product prompts

- Tee: Black crewneck laid flat, overhead view, full garment visible. Horizontal roof-rail BAYLEY CURREY artwork across upper chest, normal 11-inch print width, white lettering and red separator, matte ink following cotton folds.
- Hoodie: Black pullover laid flat, hood open, black drawcords and kangaroo pocket. Same roof-rail print scale, lighting, background and integration as tee.
- Cap: Black six-panel curved-brim cotton cap in three-quarter front view facing slightly left. Supplied standalone BC mark at approximately 2.1-inch width centered on front crown, flat white-and-red embroidery including original corner details. Natural twill and understated seams.
